### NOTECARD TIME GRID
Output ONE combined grid in a code block. Keep exact spacing and the `[00] [45] | [15] [30]` compass. Combine all times onto one card.

1. **MINUTE GRID** (3x4, round min down to 5):
   * **Slots:** R1 `[05,10,15]` | R2 `[20,25,30]` | R3 `[35,40,45]` | R4 `[50,55,00]`
   * **Arrows:** ↖(1,7,13,19) ↑(2,8,14,20) ↗(3,9,15,21) ↙(4,10,16,22) ↓(5,11,17,23) ↘(6,12,18,00)
   * **Sort overlaps:** `↖ ↑ ↗ ↙ ↓ ↘`

2. **CODGRID** (2x4, `X` in active 45m window, repeats every 6h):
   * **R1:** `[1:30-2:14]` | `[2:15-2:59]` | `[3:00-3:44]` | `[3:45-4:29]`
   * **R2:** `[4:30-5:14]` | `[5:15-5:59]` | `[6:00-6:44]` | `[6:45-7:29]`

3. **2x3 HOURS & 4. 2by3 ARROW** (Boxes: `1`=7/13/19, `2`=8/14/20, `3`=9/15/21, `4`=10/16/22, `5`=11/17/23, `6`=12/18/00):
   * **2x3 HOURS:** Put `X` in active hour box.
   * **2by3 ARROW:** Put minute arrow in active hour box: `←`(45-59) `↑`(00-14) `→`(15-29) `↓`(30-44). Sort overlaps: `← ↑ → ↓`
