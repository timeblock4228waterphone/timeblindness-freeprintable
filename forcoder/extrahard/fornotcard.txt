=== NOTECARD TIME GRID GENERATOR ===

Generate a compact notecard layout for the requested time using three grids:
- Left Side: MINUTE GRID (3x4)
- Top Right: CODGRID (2x4)
- Bottom Right: HOURS (2x3)

--------------------------------------------------
1. MINUTE GRID (Left Side - 3x4 Grid)
--------------------------------------------------
Map the hour to its directional arrow:
- ↖ = 1, 7, 13, 19
- ↑ = 2, 8, 14, 20
- ↗ = 3, 9, 15, 21
- ↙ = 4, 10, 16, 22
- ↓ = 5, 11, 17, 23
- ↘ = 6, 12, 18, 00

Round minutes down to the nearest 5-minute increment:
- 00-04 -> 00 | 05-09 -> 05 | 10-14 -> 10
- 15-19 -> 15 | 20-24 -> 20 | 25-29 -> 25
- 30-34 -> 30 | 35-39 -> 35 | 40-44 -> 40
- 45-49 -> 45 | 50-54 -> 50 | 55-59 -> 55

Grid Layout:
Row 1: [ 5  ] | [ 10 ] | [ 15 ]
Row 2: [ 20 ] | [ 25 ] | [ 30 ]
Row 3: [ 35 ] | [ 40 ] | [ 45 ]
Row 4: [ 50 ] | [ 55 ] | [ 00 ]

Rules:
- Draw ONLY the arrow inside the active minute box.
- Leave inactive boxes blank.
- MULTI-ARROW SORTING RULE: If two times share the same minute box, NEVER sort chronologically. Always sort arrows strictly in order from Step 1 to 6: ↖, ↑, ↗, ↙, ↓, ↘ (Example: [↖↘], never [↘↖]).

--------------------------------------------------
2. CODGRID (Top Right - 2x4 Grid)
--------------------------------------------------
Place an "X" in the active 45-minute window (windows repeat every 6 hours):
- Row 1: [ 1:30 ] | [ 2:15 ] | [ 3:00 ] | [ 3:45 ]
- Row 2: [ 4:30 ] | [ 5:15 ] | [ 6:00 ] | [ 6:45 ]

Window Ranges:
- 1:30 = 1:30–2:14 | 2:15 = 2:15–2:59 | 3:00 = 3:00–3:44 | 3:45 = 3:45–4:29
- 4:30 = 4:30–5:14 | 5:15 = 5:15–5:59 | 6:00 = 6:00–6:44 | 6:45 = 6:45–7:29

Rules:
- Draw an "X" in the active window box.
- Leave inactive boxes as [   ].

--------------------------------------------------
3. HOURS GRID (Bottom Right - 2x3 Grid)
--------------------------------------------------
Map the hour to its 1–6 slot (1=7/13/19, 2=8/14/20, 3=9/15/21, 4=10/16/22, 5=11/17/23, 6=12/18/00):
- Row 1: [ 1 ] | [ 2 ] | [ 3 ]
- Row 2: [ 4 ] | [ 5 ] | [ 6 ]
