 Find the Minute Block (Bottom Line of Text)

To read or write times using this layout, match the hour and the rounded minute to their designated positions. Always stack the hours directly above the minutes in each position.
• Top Left: 05, 35
• Top Middle: 10, 40
• Top Right: 15, 45
• Bottom Left: 20, 50
• Bottom Middle: 25, 55
• Bottom Right: 30, 00
